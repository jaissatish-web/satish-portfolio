Engineering notes

PV = LRV + ((mA − 4) / 16) × (URV − LRV)
  • 4 mA represents 0% of the calibrated span.
  • 20 mA represents 100% of the calibrated span.
  • Values outside the range may represent underrange, overrange, or a fault.
