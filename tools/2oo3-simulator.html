Engineering notes

Trip = (S1 ∧ S2) ∨ (S1 ∧ S3) ∨ (S2 ∧ S3)
  • Any two trip demands activate the final trip.
  • A single abnormal input does not create a process trip in this simplified model.
  • Real systems include diagnostics, bypasses, discrepancy alarms, and proof-test requirements.
