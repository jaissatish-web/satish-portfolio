How it works

Kv = Q(m³/h) × √(SG / ΔPbar); Cv = Kv / 0.865

Try this example

At 10 m³/h of water and 1 bar drop, Kv = 10 and Cv ≈ 11.561. This is a simplified coefficient estimate.
