5-point calibration

Generate rising and falling calibration points for a linear 4–20 mA pressure transmitter.

Engineering notes

mA = 4 + (Percent × 16 / 100)
  • The sequence rises from 0% to 100%, then returns to 0%.
  • Record the actual output at every point to calculate error and hysteresis.
  • Use the project tolerance and approved calibration procedure.

Use this result as an engineering reference. Confirm limits, tolerances, and acceptance criteria against the approved project documents and manufacturer data.
